## Addendum — PR 11 review remediation, 2026-10-07

The 2026-10-06 code review of PR 11 found four merge blockers against this exit: partial credit notes on taxed lines that could not post, e-invoice reconciliation that could stall or resend, converted invoices that could never carry tax, and a staging image that crashed at boot. All four, and the follow-up defects an integrated re-review found in their fixes, are fixed on the PR branch with regression tests, as is the rest of the review's findings in this PR's own code (a third round). The record, the residuals and the re-run verification (3,412 passing cases; the only failures are three Arabic-PDF cases that fail identically on `c2c3bb1` on this host; e2e 12/12) are in the [Phase 5 report](../reports/phase-5.md#pr-11-review-remediation--2026-10-07). The rules they introduce are proposed amendments to ADRs 0050 and 0053 and do not change this exit's scope.
